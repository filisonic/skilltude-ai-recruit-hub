# Blog Writing Guide for Your Client

## Easy Way to Write Blog Articles

Instead of typing markdown directly in the blog editor, use a free online tool with a visual preview!

## Recommended: StackEdit

**Website:** https://stackedit.io

### Why StackEdit?
- ✅ Free, no signup required
- ✅ Shows preview as you type
- ✅ Easy formatting buttons
- ✅ Works in any browser

## Step-by-Step Process

### 1. Write Your Article in StackEdit

1. Go to https://stackedit.io
2. Click "Start Writing"
3. Use the toolbar buttons to format:
   - **B** for bold
   - *I* for italic
   - **H** for headers
   - **List** for bullet points
4. See live preview on the right side

### 2. Copy Your Article

1. Select all text (Ctrl+A or Cmd+A)
2. Copy (Ctrl+C or Cmd+C)

### 3. Paste into Blog

1. Go to https://skilltude.com/admin/login
2. Login with your credentials
3. Click "New Article"
4. Paste the markdown into the "Content" field
5. Fill in:
   - Title
   - Excerpt (brief description)
   - Category
   - Featured Image URL (from Unsplash)
   - Tags
6. Click "Publish" or "Save Draft"

## Example Workflow

### In StackEdit, Type:

```
# The Future of AI in Recruiting

AI is transforming how companies hire. Here's what you need to know.

## Key Benefits

- Faster screening
- Better candidate matching
- Reduced bias

## Getting Started

Follow these steps:

1. Assess your needs
2. Choose tools
3. Train your team
```

### You'll See:

The right side shows exactly how it will look on your blog!

### Then:

Copy-paste into the blog editor and publish.

## Tips for Great Articles

### 1. Use Headers
Break up content with headers (## Header)

### 2. Use Lists
Bullet points are easier to read than paragraphs

### 3. Keep Paragraphs Short
2-3 sentences per paragraph

### 4. Add Bold for Emphasis
Make important points **stand out**

### 5. Use Good Images
Get free images from Unsplash.com

## Getting Unsplash Images

1. Go to https://unsplash.com
2. Search for your topic
3. Click on an image
4. Right-click the image
5. Select "Copy Image Address"
6. Paste URL in "Featured Image" field

Make sure URL starts with: `https://images.unsplash.com/`

## Common Questions

**Q: Do I need to learn markdown?**
A: No! StackEdit has buttons for everything. Just click and type.

**Q: Can I save drafts in StackEdit?**
A: Yes, it auto-saves in your browser. But always copy to the blog when done.

**Q: What if I make a mistake?**
A: You can always edit articles after publishing. Just go to admin dashboard and click edit.

**Q: Can I preview before publishing?**
A: StackEdit shows you the preview. After publishing, you can view the live article and edit if needed.

## Summary

1. Write in StackEdit (https://stackedit.io)
2. Copy the text
3. Paste into blog editor
4. Add title, image, etc.
5. Publish

This gives you a visual editor without any code changes!
